# Advanced Reel Script Structure (Gym & Lifestyle Niche)

Turns a finished reel concept (hook + angle + key points) into a locked, cinematic, timestamped shooting script. This is the last step of scripting. It assumes the ideation/research/hook work is done and translates it into a high-fidelity production blueprint, complete with exact camera movements, gym lighting cues, and rapid retention-focused cuts.

## When to run this

Only after these exist for the reel:
- The hook (the first line/visual that stops the scroll)
- The core angle or claim being made
- The rough beats/points to hit
- The CTA or ending loop

This skill is a cinematic formatter and time-boxer, not an ideation tool.

## Output Structure

Every script is a series of separate blocks, one per timestamp — never a table. Each block is its own paragraph/section with three key-value lines. Never drop a key, and never merge Voice and Visual.

The Visual line must be hyper-detailed, specifically tailored to fitness/lifestyle aesthetics, including:
- **Shot Type:** (e.g., Extreme Close-Up [ECU], Wide Shot [WS], Over-the-Shoulder [OTS], Low-Angle).
- **Camera Movement & Transitions:** (e.g., Whip pan, crash zoom, dynamic handheld tracking, smooth gimbal push-in, jump-cut, J-cut).
- **Lighting/Environment:** (e.g., Moody cinematic gym lighting, bright lifestyle daylight, lens flares, rim light on sweat).
- **Action/B-Roll:** Exact physical movements (e.g., slamming weights, mixing pre-workout, racking a barbell, walking out of a gym doors).

### Plaintext
### 0:00–0:03
**Voice:** [Verbatim spoken line or audio cue — trending sound, heavy bass drop, deep breath]
**Visual:** [Shot Type] + [Lighting/Scene] + [Camera Movement/Transition] + [Specific Action/B-Roll]. (e.g., Low-angle tracking shot. Cinematic gym lighting. Whip pan into a jump-cut as creator aggressively racks a heavy barbell. Deep focus on sweat dripping.)
**On-Screen Text:** [The text overlay shown at this timestamp — omit line if none]

*Note: Keep Voice lines punchy (~2.5–3 words per second is safe for energetic delivery). Separate each timestamp block with a blank line.*

## Standard Beat Breakdown

This is internal scaffolding used to plan timestamp ranges before writing the script blocks. Never render this to the user as a table in the final output. Maintain this order to ensure high viewer retention, ensuring visual changes (cuts, zooms, b-roll) happen at least every 3–5 seconds.

- **Hook (The Scroll-Stopper):** Visuals must be immediate and jarring. High motion, macro shots, or dynamic angles. No context needed.
- **Retention / Problem (The Anchor):** Earns the next 5 seconds. Usually a jump-cut to a wider framing or relatable gym/lifestyle b-roll.
- **Value / Payoff (The Core):** The tip, workout breakdown, or transformation. Heavily relies on b-roll inserts, split-screens, or picture-in-picture.
- **CTA / Loop (The Action):** Drives a follow/save or seamlessly visually loops back to the exact framing of the hook.

### 0:00–0:30 Timing (Short Form)
| Beat | Timestamp | Visual Pace |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Hook | 0:00–0:03 | High energy, fast zoom/pan, immediate action. |
| Retention | 0:03–0:07 | Jump-cut to steady shot or relatable b-roll. |
| Value / Payoff | 0:07–0:25 | Multiple b-roll cuts matching the spoken points. |
| CTA / Loop | 0:25–0:30 | Direct to camera, pointing to text/buttons. |

### 0:00–1:00 Timing (Long Form)
| Beat | Timestamp | Visual Pace |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Hook | 0:00–0:03 | Jarring movement or macro detail. |
| Retention | 0:03–0:10 | Shift in scenery or camera angle (e.g., from car to gym floor). |
| Value / Payoff | 0:10–0:50 | Break into 3-4 distinct visual segments/angles. |
| CTA / Loop | 0:50–1:00 | Strong eye contact, visual loop to opening frame. |

## Worked Example (0:00–0:30, Gym/Supplement Niche)

### 0:00–0:03
**Voice:** "Your creatine isn't working because you're timing it wrong."
**Visual:** Extreme Close-Up (ECU) to Medium Shot. Moody, high-contrast lighting. Crash zoom out as a tub of creatine is slammed onto a black granite locker room counter, chalk dust puffing into the air. Jump-cut to creator staring dead into the lens, slight handheld camera shake for urgency.
**On-Screen Text:** STOP TAKING CREATINE LIKE THIS 🛑

### 0:03–0:07
**Voice:** "Most guys down it right before a lift and wonder why they still look flat."
**Visual:** Whip-pan transition to B-Roll. Bright, fluorescent gym lighting. Low-angle wide shot of a gym bro dry-scooping powder into a shaker on the gym floor, looking exhausted. Quick push-in on the shaker bottle.
**On-Screen Text:** ❌ Pre-workout timing = wasted gains

### 0:07–0:15
**Voice:** "Creatine saturates your muscles over time. The pump doesn't come from taking it now, it comes from taking it daily."
**Visual:** Medium Close-Up (MCU). Back in the locker room setting, smooth gimbal movement tracking left. Creator is walking toward the camera, speaking directly to the audience. A neon glowing saturation curve graphic tracks onto the screen next to them.
**On-Screen Text:** Consistency > Timing 📈

### 0:15–0:22
**Voice:** "Take five grams every single day. Rest days, travel days, doesn't matter. Just get it in."
**Visual:** Montage sequence. Three rapid-fire jump cuts, matching the beat: 1) Macro shot of exactly one scoop of powder falling into water. 2) Creator drinking from a shaker in a lifestyle setting (kitchen, natural sunlight). 3) Creator packing a tub into a duffel bag.
**On-Screen Text:** 5g daily. No exceptions.

### 0:22–0:26
**Voice:** "That's the entire hack. Stop overthinking your stack."
**Visual:** Return to MCU locker room shot. Lighting is dramatic with a strong rim light on the creator's shoulders. Slight low angle to project authority. Creator crosses arms.
**On-Screen Text:** Keep it simple.

### 0:26–0:30
**Voice:** "Drop a save before your next push day."
**Visual:** Jump-cut to Close-Up (CU). Creator points directly down toward the bottom right of the screen (where the save icon is on IG/TikTok). Screen visually darkens slightly to make the CTA text pop.
**On-Screen Text:** 🔖 SAVE THIS FOR PUSH DAY

## Consistency Rules

- **My Style Overrides Everything:** Before formatting, read `Brain/My_Style.md` and honor its rules (e.g., preferred CTA type, "complete endings only"). Where this skill's generic guidance conflicts — such as the seamless-loop CTA beat below — `My_Style.md` wins. A visual loop back to the opening frame is fine; an incomplete trailing sentence engineered for looping is not, unless My_Style.md says otherwise.
- **Structure is Sacred:** Every reel output uses the exact block structure (Timestamp header + Voice, Visual, On-Screen Text key-value lines). No grids, no tables for the final script.
- **Visual Fidelity:** The Visual line must contain at least a shot type (e.g., Wide, Macro, CU), a camera movement/transition (e.g., Pan, Zoom, Jump-cut), and the physical action. Never write generic descriptions like "Creator talking."
- **Pacing & Movement:** In the fitness/lifestyle space, eyes get bored fast. Write a jump-cut, angle change, or b-roll insert into the visual direction at least every 3 to 5 seconds.
- **Text Rules:** Keep the On-Screen Text punchy (under 6 words). It is a visual hook, not subtitles.
- **Seamless Coverage:** Timestamp ranges must be contiguous from 0:00 to the final second with absolutely no gaps or overlaps. Scale the standard beat breakdown if total time is different from 30s or 60s.
